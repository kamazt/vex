V.E.X. v52

V.E.X. v52 rolls the completed playtest feedback into the player release.

Voice commands

  • Common damage word orders and whole numbers in digits or words are accepted.
  • Normal table conversation is ignored instead of filling the game log.
  • Negated and conditional mentions do not execute actions.
  • Genuine malformed or ambiguous action requests still receive concise feedback.

Magmaria

  • Destroyed native Magma Crystals appear in the selected Hero play area as held, out-of-play cards.
  • The pickup is stated once.
  • The held card is not an active target and does not run phase effects.
  • Destroy on a held proxy discards it to Environment trash without another destruction reaction or hand-return message.

Step reader

  • Card art remains readable in the centered reader.
  • Resolved actions stay beside the card being read.
  • Printed text remains in the normal log.
  • Routine One-Shot and trash bookkeeping no longer create reader clutter.

Target visibility

  • Printed Hero targets show a Hero target tag.
  • Effects that add target membership show the live combined classification.
  • Tags update when sources enter or leave play, cards move, or characters flip.
  • Manual tags continue to work independently.

Run the game

Extract the ZIP and double-click VEX.html. Use a modern Edge or Chrome browser. No installation or build step is required.
